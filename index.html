1. Moving Stuff Around (Alignment)

div tags for positioning layouts.

To explicitly keep things on the left:

<div align="left">
    This text stays on the left.
</div>

To center your stuff:

<div align="center">
    This text is centered.
</div>

To push things all the way to the right:

<div align="right">
    This text goes to the right.
</div>

4. Writing

Use custom styling when you want specific words or lines to stand out from the rest of the page. This helps make key notes, labels, or important phrases easier to notice.

Dropdown text is a reveal-style effect where a short heading shows first, and clicking the arrow expands the hidden content underneath.

<details>
    <summary>Click me</summary>
    This is the hidden content that appears when the dropdown opens.
</details>

You can change the color of a single sentence or word by wrapping it in a styled block:

<span style="color:#00AC60;">This text is custom green</span>

If you want a more script-style approach, you can also use color markup like this:

$\color{#00AC60}{\text{This text is custom green}}$
